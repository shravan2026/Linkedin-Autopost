Working in a CRO vs Sponsor Company – My Experience With Both

Having spent 5+ years at Syneos Health (CRO) and now working at Bristol Myers Squibb (Sponsor), I'm often asked: which is better?

The honest answer: both have distinct advantages, and the "right" choice depends on your career stage and goals.

🏢 CRO Experience (Syneos Health):
- Exposure to multiple therapeutic areas and document types
- Fast-paced environment that builds adaptability
- Working across different sponsors means learning varied SOPs and expectations
- Excellent for building a broad skill set early in your career

🏢 Sponsor Experience (Bristol Myers Squibb):
- Deep therapeutic expertise — you live and breathe the molecule
- Greater involvement in strategic decisions
- Continuity from protocol development through submission
- Stronger sense of ownership over the product lifecycle

Key differences I've noticed:

In a CRO, you're a versatile problem-solver working across accounts. At a sponsor, you're a subject matter expert embedded in the program.

CRO work taught me speed, flexibility, and how to manage multiple stakeholders. Sponsor work taught me depth, strategic thinking, and long-term program vision.

My advice to medical writers considering a switch: if you've spent your career in one setting, try the other. The perspective you gain is invaluable.

There's no wrong path — only different learning curves.
