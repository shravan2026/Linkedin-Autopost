Pharmacovigilance is the most underrated career in pharma — and medical writers are perfectly positioned to pivot into it.

Here's a reality check:

→ Global pharmacovigilance market: $8.2 billion in 2024, projected $15.9 billion by 2030
→ Post-COVID, regulatory agencies worldwide mandated stronger safety monitoring
→ FDA received 2.4 million adverse event reports in 2023 alone
→ Every single one requires documentation, assessment, and narrative writing

Yet when pharmacy students think "pharma career," PV is rarely their first choice. That's a mistake.

Why pharmacovigilance is booming:

1. REGULATORY PRESSURE IS INCREASING
→ EU's GVP modules keep expanding requirements
→ FDA's FAERS system demands real-time safety signal documentation
→ India's PvPI (Pharmacovigilance Programme of India) is maturing rapidly

2. THE WRITING IS SUBSTANTIAL
→ Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs) — millions annually
→ Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSURs/PBRERs) — complex narratives
→ Risk Management Plans (RMPs) — strategic documents
→ Signal detection reports, benefit-risk assessments, DSUR

3. AI IS AUGMENTING, NOT REPLACING
→ AI handles case processing triage
→ Human writers still needed for narrative quality, causality assessment, regulatory strategy

4. COMPENSATION IS COMPETITIVE
→ PV writers in India: ₹12-30 LPA (mid to senior)
→ PV writers in US: $90K-$140K
→ Demand consistently outstrips supply

For medical writers, the transition is natural:

You already understand regulatory frameworks, medical terminology, and document standards. PV writing adds safety science expertise — and opens a parallel career track with massive growth.

My advice: If you're a medical writer feeling plateaued, explore CIOMS forms, MedDRA coding, and signal detection. The PV world needs your skills.
