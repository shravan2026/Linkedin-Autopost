CDSCO approved 204 new drugs in 2024. The FDA approved 50.

Read that again.

While the global pharma world obsesses over FDA timelines, India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation has been quietly transforming itself into one of the fastest regulatory agencies on the planet.

Here's what's changed in the last 3 years:

→ New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules 2019 fully operationalized
→ Average approval timeline reduced from 18-24 months to 8-12 months for many categories
→ SUGAM portal digitized the entire submission process
→ Dedicated fast-track pathways for orphan drugs, breakthrough therapies, and pandemic drugs
→ India-specific Phase III waivers for drugs already approved in regulated markets

The numbers speak for themselves:
• Clinical trial approvals: 14-day timeline for applications with no queries
• Emergency Use Authorizations during COVID processed in under 2 weeks
• New Drug Applications moving at 2x the speed compared to 2019

Why is nobody talking about this?

Because the narrative hasn't caught up with reality. People still think of CDSCO as it was in 2015 — slow, opaque, and unpredictable.

But global sponsors are noticing. India's clinical trial registrations are up 34% since 2022. MNCs like Novartis, Roche, and Sanofi are increasing their India regulatory submissions.

As someone who works with regulatory documents daily, I've seen the shift firsthand. The quality expectations have risen, but so has the efficiency. That's a win for patients who get access to medicines faster.
