Project Management Skills Every Medical Writer Needs

Here's something they don't teach you in pharmacy school: medical writing is as much about project management as it is about writing.

After managing complex document deliverables across global teams at Bristol Myers Squibb and Syneos Health, here are the PM skills I consider non-negotiable:

🔹 **Timeline management** — Regulatory submissions don't wait. You need to work backwards from filing dates, build in buffer for reviews, and know when to escalate delays.

🔹 **Stakeholder management** — You'll work with 10+ reviewers on a single document. Knowing who needs to approve what — and in what order — prevents bottlenecks.

🔹 **Scope control** — "Can you also add this section?" Scope creep is real. Learn to assess impact, communicate trade-offs, and document change requests.

🔹 **Risk anticipation** — Data locks delayed? Statistical outputs behind schedule? Anticipate downstream impacts on your writing timeline and communicate proactively.

🔹 **Meeting facilitation** — Kick-off meetings, comment resolution calls, alignment discussions. A medical writer who can run an efficient meeting is invaluable.

🔹 **Tool proficiency** — Whether it's Veeva Vault, SharePoint, or project trackers — master the tools your organization uses for document workflows.

**The reality:** The most technically skilled writer will struggle without project management discipline. In our world, delivering quality on time is the standard — not the exception.

Invest in these skills. Your career will thank you.
